Droynis checks the security and privacy settings of your phone, explains every finding in plain words, shows the raw evidence and opens the Settings screen that fixes it. It never changes anything itself.

It is not an antivirus: it audits how the phone is set up and looks for signs adware leaves, not for malicious code.

<b>What it checks</b>
<ul>
<li>Device integrity: patches, encryption, bootloader, root, SELinux</li>
<li>Access control: screen lock, biometrics, USB debugging, SIM PIN</li>
<li>Apps and permissions: accessibility, device admins, sideloaded apps</li>
<li>Suspicious apps: hidden apps, background camera/mic use, trojan permissions</li>
<li>Network: Private DNS, VPN, Wi-Fi, certificates, 2G, Bluetooth, NFC</li>
</ul>

<b>Honest results</b>
A check passes only on a value it actually read; otherwise it is Unknown. A 0–100 score sums it up.

<b>Private by design</b>
No internet permission, no Google Play Services, no analytics.
